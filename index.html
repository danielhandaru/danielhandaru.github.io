SO WHO AM I?

I'm Daniel Handaru, full-stack product designer

A Sydney-based designer and musician with a focus on digital product strategy, design systems, UX research and personalisation. I provide design solutions for digital transformation projects, ecommerce, SaaS products and Enterprise CMS builds.
